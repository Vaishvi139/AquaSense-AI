Limitations to Understand

⚠️

Predictions Depend on Training Data : The model's accuracy depends on the quality, size, and representativeness of its training dataset. Results may be less reliable for samples unlike the training data.

pH Prediction Is Not a Safety Certification : AquaSense AI estimates pH; it does not test for all harmful microorganisms, chemicals, or contaminants. A prediction cannot establish that water is safe to drink. Appropriate laboratory testing is necessary to assess drinking-water safety.
