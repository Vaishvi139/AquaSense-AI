2. Random Forest Regression

pĤ = (1/N) × Σ pĤᵢ

Random Forest combines predictions from multiple decision trees. Its final prediction is the average of the individual tree predictions.

3. HistGradientBoosting

Final prediction = initial prediction + learned tree updates

Successive trees learn patterns that improve earlier predictions. Their combined output produces the final predicted pH value.

This is a conceptual explanation, not a display of the model's exact internal tree contributions.
