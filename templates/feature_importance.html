Understanding Feature Importance

Why Is Feature Importance Important?

Machine learning models learn patterns from training data. Feature importance helps us interpret those patterns and understand which input variables contribute most according to a particular model and importance calculation method.

  • Helps interpret model behaviour.
  • Identifies features the model relies on most.
  • Supports comparison and evaluation of model inputs.
  • Helps guide further investigation and model improvement.
  • Makes machine learning results easier to explain in presentations.

Importance of Water Quality Parameters

AquaSense AI uses five input parameters to predict pH. Each parameter describes a different characteristic of water.

🌡️

Temperature

Water temperature, recorded in °C. Measures how warm or cold the water is. Temperature can affect water chemistry and pH measurements.

🌫️

Turbidity

A measure of how cloudy the water is. Indicates water cloudiness caused by suspended particles. It provides information about the physical condition of water.

🧂

Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)

Total dissolved solids, commonly measured in mg/L. Represents the concentration of dissolved substances in water. It helps describe the water's chemical composition.

⚡

Electrical Conductivity

Indicates the water's ability to conduct electricity. Measures how readily water conducts electricity. It is often related to the concentration of dissolved ions.

🫧

Dissolved Oxygen

The amount of oxygen dissolved in the water. Measures the oxygen dissolved in water. It is an important indicator of aquatic environmental conditions.

How to Interpret the Feature Importance Chart

  • A higher score indicates greater importance according to the method used to calculate that score.
  • A lower score indicates less importance under that same method.
  • Importance scores do not automatically show whether a parameter increases or decreases predicted pH.
  • Importance does not prove that a parameter causes a change in pH.

Note: Feature importance depends on the model and calculation method. Scores from different methods should not automatically be treated as directly comparable.

Role in AquaSense AI

In AquaSense AI, feature importance provides additional insight into the water-quality parameters used by the trained prediction model. It complements the predicted pH values and model performance metrics, helping users better understand the analysis.

Feature importance is an interpretation aid, not a water-safety test. Drinking-water safety requires appropriate laboratory testing.
